# Root-Cause Mapping — From Permitted Surface to Hidden Depth, And to Fruits That Outlive the Season

The amateur analyst looks at headlines and thinks headlines drive events. The professional analyst sees that headlines are what deep processes **permit** to be shown, and that the visible event is consequence, not cause.

But there is one more level that even most professional analysts miss: **fruits**. The leaves are decoration — they catch light, do their synthesis, and fall when the season ends. The fruits are why the whole tree exists. They are durable, they nourish, they contain seeds that grow new trees.

In analytical terms: **fruits are the durable outputs of the root system**, the things that outlive any particular news cycle and continue to produce value (or damage, or new dependencies) for years to decades. They are also where investment value lives — because investments are always purchases of fruits or of seeds.

This skill maps the topology across all five levels. It is the foundation for any serious forecast.

## Prerequisites

- `awareness-frame` completed
- 12-layer deconstruction completed or in progress
- Willingness to think across multiple time scales — leaves operate on weeks, fruits on years to decades
- Recognition that fruits and seeds require different treatment than leaves and branches

## Core principle

> Five levels, three time scales, two directions. Roots, stems, branches, leaves are the topology for understanding causation (bottom-up). Roots, stems, branches, fruits are the topology for projecting results (also bottom-up but through a different terminal). Leaves are diagnostic. Fruits are purpose.

A forecast that projects only future leaves is a forecast of permitted surface — useful for monitoring, useless for decisions that matter beyond the news cycle. A forecast that projects fruits answers the question that actually matters: **what will this root system produce that will keep producing value (or damage) long after this season ends?**

## The five-level topology with environmental context

```
LEAVES (observable events, present tense)
  Time scale: minutes to weeks
  Visibility: high — this reaches the analyst directly
  Function: do photosynthesis, signal what the tree is doing this season
  Lifespan: short, fall predictably each season

BRANCHES (programs and campaigns)
  Time scale: weeks to quarters
  Visibility: medium
  Function: hold leaves and bear fruits where conditions are right

STEM (strategic course, the trunk)
  Time scale: years
  Visibility: low — must be inferred from accumulated branches
  Function: structural support, distribution channel for resources between roots and crown

ROOTS (deep processes)
  Time scale: decades, sometimes centuries
  Visibility: near zero direct — inferred from pattern of stems over time
  Function: extract resources from the substrate, anchor the whole system

FRUITS (durable outputs, what the system produces)
  Time scale: years to decades
  Visibility: high once produced, but easily mistaken for leaves
  Function: contain seeds (rebirth), provide nourishment (value transfer), outlive the season
  Distinction from leaves: a leaf falls and rots; a fruit feeds something or grows into something
```

**ENVIRONMENTAL CONTEXT (the substrate, the climate, the diseases)**

The tree does not float in space. It grows in soil, under climate, surrounded by external forces and internal threats. These environmental factors determine whether a healthy root system actually produces healthy fruits — or whether even a strong-looking tree is hollowing from inside.

```
SOIL (substrate the roots draw from)
  Composition: capital, energy, information, time horizon, social legitimacy
  Quality: rich / sufficient / depleted / toxic
  Trajectory: enriching / stable / depleting / collapsing
  Effect on tree: depleted soil starves even strong roots over time

CLIMATE (macro environment around the tree)
  Season: which long phase the tree exists in (favorable / neutral / hostile)
  Active weather: current macro forces (storms, droughts, calm)
  Climate trend: becoming friendlier / stable / becoming hostile

EXTERNAL DESTRUCTIVE FORCES (winds and storms)
  Forces that can break branches or strip leaves rapidly
  Examples: regulatory shocks, competitor attacks, sanctions waves, market crashes
  Resilience of tree to each

INTERNAL DESTRUCTIVE PROCESSES (diseases and parasites)
  Processes that hollow the tree from inside while external indicators look normal
  Diseases: institutional decay, ideological exhaustion, talent erosion, financial dependence
  Parasites: actors feeding off the system without contributing (corrupt officials, rent-seekers,
    fifth columns, captured regulators)
  Detection: requires specific diagnostic indicators (see system-health-diagnostics skill)
```

**Critical insight.** A tree can have:
- Strong roots + good soil + favorable climate + no diseases = thriving
- Strong roots + depleted soil = slow decline despite root strength
- Strong roots + good soil + hostile climate = stunted despite system health
- Strong roots + good soil + favorable climate + active disease = sudden collapse from inside

The most-missed scenario is the last. Externally everything looks fine; metrics report normal; only specific internal indicators reveal the disease — and by the time external manifestation occurs, intervention may be too late.

**Critical distinction.** A leaf and a fruit can both be "visible events that happened in the past year." The difference is what they do afterward:

- A press conference is a leaf (informational, transient, no continuing production)
- A signed defense contract for $50B over 10 years is a fruit (continues producing value to the contractor for a decade)
- A daily oil price quote is a leaf
- A pipeline that begins operations and serves flows for 30 years is a fruit
- A research paper announcement is a leaf
- A patent that becomes the foundation for a $100B industry is a fruit (also a seed)

## The procedure

The procedure works **bottom-up for analysis** (leaves to roots, then roots to fruits) and **top-down for forecast** (roots forward to future fruits).

### Step 1 — Inventory the leaves

From deconstruction layers (especially Layers 1, 2, 3), collect visible events. List 10-30. Patterns reveal themselves only with sufficient density.

For each leaf, note:
- What happened
- When (date)
- Who (actor)
- What was claimed as reason
- What was unusual

### Step 2 — Cluster leaves into branches

Group leaves by apparent coordination or shared trajectory. Each branch should have a directional pattern, implicit or explicit objectives, a rough timeline. Aim for 3-7 branches.

### Step 3 — Identify stems (strategic courses)

For each branch or group, ask what strategic position must exist to allow this branch to grow. Stems are inferred:

- Name explicitly (one sentence)
- When likely adopted
- What triggered adoption
- Committed actor(s)

Aim for 1-3 stems per major question.

### Step 4 — Trace stems to roots

For each stem, ask what deep process gave rise to it and permits it to grow. Roots come in identifiable categories:

**Cyclical roots:** debt cycle (Dalio ~75-100 years), geopolitical hegemonic cycle (~80-100 years), technological paradigm cycle (~50 years), generational psychological cycle (~80 years).

**Structural roots:** demographics, geography, energy and material flows, institutional histories.

**Power-position roots:** competitive position trajectories, internal coherence, capability distribution.

**Psychological-historical roots:** generational traumas, civilizational narratives, personal psychology of decision-makers.

Aim for 2-4 roots per question.

### Step 5 — Identify fruits

This is the level most analysts miss. For the root-stem-branch system identified, ask: **what is this system actually producing that will outlive the current season?**

Fruits are characterized by:

**Durability** — they continue to exist and produce value beyond the immediate event window. A 10-year defense contract continues to produce revenue for the contractor regardless of next year's headlines.

**Autonomous value** — once produced, they have value independent of the system that produced them. A pipeline's flows continue regardless of the political climate that authorized the pipeline.

**Seeding capacity** — the most consequential fruits contain seeds: they enable new systems. A breakthrough patent enables a new industry. A precedent (legal or geopolitical) enables future actions that were previously inadmissible.

**Beneficiary specificity** — fruits feed someone specific. Identifying the beneficiary by name (not abstraction) is essential. "Defense industry" is not a beneficiary; "Lockheed Martin's Skunk Works program" is.

For each major branch, ask: **what fruits does this branch produce?**

For each fruit, document:
- Name and brief description
- Beneficiary (specific actor)
- Time horizon of value extraction (years to decades)
- Dependencies (what conditions must hold for the fruit to be harvested)
- Seeding capacity (does this fruit also seed a new system? if yes, what?)

### Step 6 — Distinguish fruits from seeds

Fruits and seeds are related but distinct. A fruit feeds — it transfers value to a beneficiary. A seed plants — it can grow into a new tree. Some outputs are both (a major patent is a fruit for the inventor and a seed for an industry).

For each fruit, ask: **does it contain a seed?**

If yes, identify:
- What new system could grow from this seed
- Required conditions for germination (timing, environment)
- Time horizon for the new tree to grow

Seeds are typically the most valuable outputs because they compound — they become roots of new trees that produce their own fruits.

### Step 7 — Map environmental context

Now characterize the environment in which this tree grows. Even a perfect topology (roots → fruits) produces poor predictions if environmental factors are missed.

**Soil assessment:**
For each root, identify what substrate it draws from. Then characterize:
- Composition: which resources are flowing into the system?
- Quality: rich / sufficient / depleted / toxic
- Trajectory: enriching / stable / depleting / collapsing
- Specific concerns: are any critical substrate elements becoming scarce?

Example: a regime drawing on substrate of (oil revenues + military legitimacy + administrative capacity) — what is the trajectory of each? If oil revenues stable but legitimacy eroding and admin capacity declining, the substrate is depleting heterogeneously.

**Climate assessment:**
- Current season: where in long cycles is the system operating?
- Active weather: what macro forces are currently impacting?
- Climate trend: where is the macro environment heading?

This overlaps with the macro phase identification in awareness-frame, but here it's specifically about the environment of THIS tree, not the general macro picture.

**External destructive forces (winds and storms):**
List forces that could break branches or strip leaves rapidly. For each:
- What is it?
- How active is it now?
- What is the tree's resilience?
- What would a major event of this type do?

Examples for political regime: sudden sanctions escalation, military setback, public protest cascade, succession crisis.

Examples for corporation: regulatory action, key talent departure, competitive product launch, supply chain disruption.

### Step 8 — Identify diseases and parasites

This is the most-missed analytical category. Internal destructive processes hollow the tree while external indicators appear normal.

**Diseases (internal decay processes):**
- Institutional coercion: formal procedures increasingly diverge from real practice
- Talent erosion: top level held by inertia, middle decaying, bottom emigrating
- Bureaucratic sclerosis: complexity rising without productivity gain
- Ideological exhaustion: official narratives stop motivating even loyalists
- Financial dependency: growing reliance on extraordinary measures for normal function

**Parasites (external actors extracting without contributing):**
- Corruption networks: officials extracting rent rather than delivering function
- Rent-seekers: actors capturing value flows without adding production
- Fifth columns: actors aligned with hostile foreign interests in key positions
- Captured regulators: institutions ostensibly governing while serving regulated
- Vampires: financial structures extracting value from productive activity

For each disease/parasite:
- Is it active in the current system? (Yes / suspected / no / unknown)
- What indicators would confirm it?
- What is its current intensity?
- What is its trajectory?

If disease/parasite analysis reveals significant concerns, the `system-health-diagnostics` skill should be loaded for full diagnosis. The mapping itself just flags the presence of triggers.

### Step 9 — Test the topology

**Permission test:** could these leaves have appeared 10 years ago? If no, what changed in the roots?

**Counter-test:** what events should NOT appear if your mapping is correct?

**Fruit test:** for each identified fruit, is it actually durable, or is it a long-named leaf?
Test: imagine the current political/economic environment changes drastically. Does the fruit still produce value? If yes, it is a fruit. If no, it was a leaf with a long timeline.

**Beneficiary test:** can you name the specific actor that eats each fruit? If beneficiary is abstract ("the financial system," "society"), the fruit may be misidentified — refine to specific entities.

**Timeline plausibility:** roots operate on decades, stems on years, branches on quarters, leaves on weeks, fruits on years to decades. If timescales don't match the level, reclassify.

### Step 10 — Output

Produce the map. Format below. This becomes the input for `decision-tree-forecast` (which projects future fruits, not future leaves).

## Worked example

**Subject:** US-Israel war on Iran (Operation Epic Fury), considering investment implications.

### Leaves (recent observable):

- Initial strikes February 28, 2026
- Ceasefire April 8, 2026
- Defense ETF (ITA) -12% during peak conflict
- Gold -20% from peak
- Brent oil $115-126 range
- Trump's "unconditional surrender" rhetoric March 2026
- Hormuz blockade ongoing
- Pakistan-mediated talks no progress
- War Powers Resolution deadline May 1, 2026

### Branches:

**Branch 1: Direct military operation**
- Strikes, decapitation, intelligence operations
- Replacement of munitions consumed (850+ Tomahawks)

**Branch 2: Information operation around the war**
- Multiple official narratives, deception, deadlines
- "Imminent threat" framing

**Branch 3: Economic warfare layer**
- Hormuz blockade, secondary sanctions, financial pressure
- $580M oil short before announcement (insider trading)

**Branch 4: Coalition coordination**
- Saudi/UAE/Israel coordination
- Spain refusal — NATO fissures

### Stems:

**Stem A: "Restore American hegemonic posture in Middle East"**
- Adopted: probably late 2024 with Trump transition
- Trigger: campaign commitments to Saudi/Israeli interests + economic shock therapy needs
- Committed actor: Trump administration coordination with regional partners

**Stem B: "Maintain regional balance favorable to Saudi/Israel without ground invasion"**
- Adopted: post-October 7 Hamas attack period (2023-24)
- Trigger: Iran proxy network demonstrated reach
- Committed actor: Israeli + Saudi/UAE coalition

**Stem C: "Use military operations as economic policy instrument"**
- Adopted: post-2008 implicitly, intensified post-2020
- Trigger: structural exhaustion of conventional monetary tools
- Committed actor: US administration regardless of party

### Roots:

**Root 1: Late-stage hegemonic transition (geopolitical cycle)**
- Time horizon: decades
- Effect: forces declining hegemon to demonstrate reach episodically
- Why now: hegemon-power transition in active phase, 2020s

**Root 2: Late-stage debt cycle for fiat dollar (debt cycle)**
- Time horizon: decades
- Effect: makes military operations attractive as economic shock
- Why now: cycle phase requires distraction from fiscal pressure

**Root 3: Saudi-Iranian regional rivalry (structural)**
- Time horizon: decades-long
- Effect: creates standing demand for US engagement against Iran
- Why now: Saudi succession (MBS) generation seeking regional dominance

**Root 4: US defense industrial base requirement for active conflict (structural)**
- Time horizon: decade-plus
- Effect: military-industrial complex requires periodic conflict for economic logic
- Why now: post-Cold-War long peace had reduced defense spending; ramp needs trigger

### Fruits (durable outputs):

**Fruit 1: Multi-decade Pentagon procurement contracts for munitions replacement**
- Beneficiaries: RTX (Tomahawks), LMT (THAAD systems), NOC (B-2 ops support, ISR)
- Time horizon: 10-20 years of revenue
- Dependencies: maintained Pentagon budget, no major treaty restrictions
- Seeding capacity: yes — funded R&D leads to next-generation systems (PrSM, hypersonics)

**Fruit 2: Precedent of US Navy used for sectoral commodity blockade (Hormuz)**
- Beneficiaries (positive): future US administrations seeking economic coercion options
- Beneficiaries (negative): BRICS+ accelerated dedollarization
- Time horizon: decades-long doctrinal effect
- Dependencies: precedent not reversed by international institutional response
- Seeding capacity: yes — seeds the BRICS+ alternative settlement systems (likely accelerated by 2-3 years)

**Fruit 3: Saudi-Israeli regional dominance configuration**
- Beneficiaries: MBS personally, House of Saud, Netanyahu coalition, UAE leadership
- Time horizon: 10+ years
- Dependencies: continued US backing or at least non-interference
- Seeding capacity: medium — could seed Abraham Accords expansion to other Arab states

**Fruit 4: Decapitation of Iranian leadership (Khamenei killed)**
- Beneficiaries: Iranian internal pragmatic factions, Saudi/Israel
- Time horizon: 5-10 years of restructured Iranian politics
- Dependencies: succession outcome (son in power, but more pragmatic positions)
- Seeding capacity: yes — could seed Iran-Saudi normalization (low-but-non-zero probability path)

**Fruit 5: Insider profit on $580M oil short (specific identified)**
- Beneficiary: unidentified actor with material non-public information
- Time horizon: already realized
- Dependencies: realization complete
- Seeding capacity: zero — pure value extraction

**Fruit 6: Erosion of US treaty alliance trust (Spain refusal, NATO fissures)**
- Beneficiaries (negative): future US administrations face higher coordination costs
- Beneficiaries (positive): alternative alliance structures (CSTO, SCO) gain relative attractiveness
- Time horizon: 5-15 years for full re-alignment
- Dependencies: trust erosion not reversed by future US administrations
- Seeding capacity: yes — seeds multi-polar alliance architecture

### Topology verification:

**Permission test:** could decapitation strike on supreme leader of Iran have happened in 2010? Almost certainly no — would have triggered massive global backlash. What changed in roots: hegemonic transition normalized previously-unthinkable actions; debt-cycle pressure increased tolerance for shock; Saudi-Israeli coordination matured.

**Fruit test:** is "10-year Pentagon contract" a fruit or a long-named leaf? Test: if next administration is anti-war, do the contracts continue? Yes, because they're under signed agreements with specific deliverables. Fruit confirmed.

**Beneficiary test:** can we name specific beneficiaries of each fruit? Yes — RTX, LMT, NOC, MBS, Netanyahu, etc. Specific.

**Counter-test:** if mapping correct, should NOT see (a) full Iranian regime collapse — Stem B is balance, not regime change; (b) Russian active military intervention in Iran — Russia is too constrained; (c) US public opinion shift to anti-war forcing pullout before fruits ripen.

### Conclusion

The dominant root processes are hegemonic transition, debt-cycle pressure, and Saudi-Israeli regional rivalry. The current stems serve these roots with branches that produce six identifiable fruits across multiple time horizons. The most durable and seed-capable fruits are: (1) decade-long Pentagon contracts feeding RTX/LMT/NOC; (2) precedent of commodity blockade accelerating BRICS+ dedollarization; (3) Saudi-Israeli regional dominance.

For investment purposes: defense procurement fruits are **already in the tree, ripening on contracts already signed**. The dedollarization fruit is more abstract but represents a 10-year structural shift. Saudi-Israeli configuration is largely priced in but underweighted in models that haven't accounted for full duration.

## Anti-patterns

- **Treating leaves as fruits.** "We saw a 30% rise in defense ETFs during the war" — this could be a leaf (transient war pricing) or a fruit (durable revaluation reflecting backlog growth). Test: does the value persist after war ends? If yes, fruit. If no, leaf.
- **Inventing roots that aren't structural.** "Trump is bellicose" is not a root. Root processes change over decades; personality traits are not processes.
- **Single-root analysis.** Real situations have 2-4 roots typically. "It's all because of debt cycle" misses geopolitical and structural elements.
- **Fruits without specific beneficiaries.** "The defense industry benefits" — too abstract. Name specific companies, specific programs, specific revenues.
- **Missing seeding capacity.** Identifying a fruit but not asking whether it contains a seed for a new system. The most consequential investment opportunities live in seeds that the analyst initially classified as "just a fruit."
- **Confusing fruits with leaves over short observation windows.** Looking at one news cycle, every output looks like a leaf. Fruits become apparent only when you hold the time horizon longer (years).
- **Skipping the permission test.** Without it, you'll mistake old patterns for new and miss recent root shifts that permit previously-impossible fruits.

## Integration with deconstruction protocol

This skill operates between **Phase 2 (deconstruction layers)** and **Phase 3 (Staff Block)**. The 12 layers provide raw material — leaves and some branches. This skill organizes that material vertically into the topology, all the way down to roots, and identifies the fruits that the system is producing.

Specifically:
- `forces-and-potentials` measures forces operating at root level (forces measured at leaf level are derivative)
- `strategic-maneuvers` operate at stem level (you can shift the stem; you cannot shift the roots quickly)
- `decision-tree-forecast` projects forward to **future fruits** (not future leaves) — leaves are diagnostic, fruits are the goal of forecasting

Output is stored in Analysis record under `rootCauseMap` field with explicit fruits subsection. Verification at horizon date checks: did the predicted fruits emerge from the identified roots? Did the seeds we identified begin to germinate? This is the deepest learning — and the most valuable for investment-relevant analysis.
