# Decision Tree Forecast — Projecting Future Fruits From Present Roots

The old forecast format listed scenarios at horizon dates with probabilities. This works for simple monitoring but loses the structural insight that matters for serious decisions.

The new format projects through the topology established in `root-cause-mapping`: from current roots forward through evolving stems and branches to **future fruits**. The forecast does not ask "what will happen?" — it asks "**given these roots, what fruits will this system produce, when, for whom, and what seeds might these fruits contain?**"

This is the difference between forecasting weather and forecasting harvests. Weather is leaves. Harvests are fruits. Investment-relevant forecasting is harvest forecasting.

## Prerequisites

This is the most prerequisite-heavy skill in the entire ОСП toolkit. It is the synthesis point of all earlier work:

- `awareness-frame` completed
- 12-layer deconstruction completed
- `root-cause-mapping` completed (5 levels with fruits and seeds)
- `forces-and-potentials` completed (potentials measured at root level)
- `strategic-maneuvers` completed (subject options identified)
- `two-paths-synthesis` completed (convergence/divergence analyzed)

If any prerequisite is missing, the forecast loses topological grounding and degrades to leaf-extrapolation.

## Core principle

> Future fruits are seeded by current roots. Forecast is the projection of which fruits ripen, which abort, which seeds germinate, and which seeds rot. Probability weights at each level reflect not only force differentials but also convergence between formal logic and game-theoretic paths.

The mistake is to extrapolate leaves linearly — "X happened, Y happened, therefore Z will happen." This works only when roots are stable. When roots shift, leaf-extrapolation fails because it has no model of the shift. Topology-aware forecast captures the shift naturally because it tracks the root level explicitly and projects through the structure.

## The procedure

### Step 1 — Restate roots and current stems

From `root-cause-mapping` output, restate roots and stems. This is a sanity check — does the analyst still believe these are the roots? If new evidence has appeared since mapping, update.

If roots are wrong, forecast will be wrong. So this restatement matters.

### Step 2 — Project root evolution

For each root, project its trajectory at multiple horizons:

- **3 months ahead:** how has this root moved? Roots are slow but not static — debt cycle progresses, demographics shift, decision-makers age, geopolitical phases advance
- **12 months ahead:** larger root movement
- **36 months ahead:** substantial root movement
- **5-10 year horizons** (where relevant for fruit ripening)

Each projection should note:
- Direction (root strengthening / weakening / phase-shifting)
- Confidence (how predictable is this trajectory)
- Possible disruption events (what could discontinuously change this root)

### Step 3 — Project stem evolution from roots

For each current stem, given projected root evolution, project stem evolution:

- **Strengthening** — supporting roots growing → stem accelerates
- **Persisting** — roots stable → stem continues current trajectory
- **Weakening** — supporting roots eroding → stem decays
- **Transforming** — roots shift → stem must adapt or be replaced
- **Replaced** — roots shift fundamentally → new stem grows in place of old

For each stem, identify the time horizon when its evolution becomes consequential.

### Step 4 — Project branches from stems

For each evolving stem, project branch evolution:

- New branches: stem requires new programs to maintain itself or pursue evolved objectives
- Continuing branches: programs that work continue
- Withering branches: programs no longer serving the stem wind down
- Replacement branches: failed branches replaced by new ones serving same stem

### Step 5 — Project fruits

This is the central step. From projected branches, identify what fruits will ripen, when, for whom, with what probability.

For each fruit (existing or projected):

**Ripening trajectory:**
- Already ripe (producing value now)
- Ripening (will produce more value as supporting branches mature)
- Forming (early stage, will become ripe in 1-3 years)
- Bud (very early, may form into fruit if conditions hold)
- Aborting (was forming but underlying branches are weakening)

**Probability of full ripening:**
- For fruits already ripe — probability of continued production
- For ripening fruits — probability they reach full maturity
- For forming fruits — probability they form fully
- For buds — probability they become fruits

Probabilities should reflect convergence/divergence from `two-paths-synthesis`:
- Convergent prediction (both paths agree) → higher confidence, narrower probability range
- Divergent prediction → wider range or branch-point in tree

**Time horizon:** when is the fruit harvestable / value-extractable?

**Beneficiary:** specific named actor who eats this fruit. Specific.

**Conditional dependencies:** what must hold for the fruit to ripen?

### Step 6 — Identify seeds within fruits

For each fruit projected to ripen, ask: does it contain a seed?

For each seed:

**Germination probability:** will the seed actually grow into a new tree?

**Germination conditions:** what environment is required (timing, complementary factors, lack of disruption)?

**Time to maturity:** how long until the new tree produces its own fruits?

**Investment characteristics of seeds:**
- Seeds are typically more asymmetric than fruits — lower probability of success but exponentially larger payoff if successful
- Seeds in early stages are often invisible to most analysts
- Seeds within winning fruits are often the highest-value finds

This step is where the most asymmetric investment opportunities are identified. A seed correctly identified at germination stage is the analytical equivalent of buying Bitcoin in 2010 or defense stocks before Iran 2026.

### Step 7 — Build the projection tree

Now organize all projections into a tree structure. The tree grows **bottom-up** — roots at base, fruits and seeds at top — but each node has probability weights reflecting uncertainty.

Tree structure:

```
ROOTS (with projected evolution at 3mo / 12mo / 36mo / 5-10yr)
   │
   ▼ (each root supports 1-3 stems)
   │
STEMS (with projected evolution)
   │
   ▼ (each stem supports 1-4 branches)
   │
BRANCHES (with projected evolution)
   │
   ▼ (each branch produces 0-3 fruits)
   │
FRUITS (with ripening trajectory and probability)
   │
   ▼ (some fruits contain seeds)
   │
SEEDS (with germination probability and conditions)
```

At each level, probability weights are assigned. A fruit's probability of ripening is the product of:
- Probability its supporting branches continue (from Step 4)
- Probability its supporting stem persists (from Step 3)
- Probability its underlying roots maintain trajectory (from Step 2)
- Conditional dependencies specific to the fruit

### Step 8 — Identify divergence branching

From `two-paths-synthesis`, recall divergent points (where formal and game-theoretic paths disagreed). At these points, the tree explicitly branches into two sub-trees, each with its own probability:

```
[At divergence point]
  ├── Path A predicts: <branch projection> (probability X%)
  │     └── Future fruits under Path A
  └── Path B predicts: <branch projection> (probability 100-X%)
        └── Future fruits under Path B
```

Convergent points need no branching — they're trunk of the tree.

### Step 9 — Generate falsifiable indicators

For each significant projection (each fruit, each seed, each divergence resolution), define **specific binary indicators** with dates:

- "Indicator: ITA defense ETF +15% over current level by December 31, 2026" → confirms Defense Fruit 1 ripening
- "Indicator: BRICS+ joint announcement of alternative oil settlement currency by June 30, 2027" → confirms Dedollarization Seed germinating
- "Indicator: Russian fiscal deficit Q3 2026 widens beyond 4 trillion ruble" → confirms Russian Constraint Root continuing trajectory

Indicators are the verification mechanism — they get archived with the analysis and trigger the verification cycle when their dates arrive.

### Step 10 — Produce output

Final output is the projection tree with probability weights, time horizons, fruits, seeds, indicators. Format below.

## Worked example

**Subject:** US-Israel-Iran war, investment implications, 5-year horizon.

### Roots (from root-cause-mapping):
- R1: Late-stage hegemonic transition (decade horizon, active phase)
- R2: Late-stage debt cycle for fiat dollar (decade horizon, late phase)
- R3: Saudi-Iranian rivalry (decade-long, MBS generation in active phase)
- R4: US defense industrial base requirement (decade-plus, requires periodic conflict)

### Root projections:
- R1 at 3mo: continues active phase, no significant change
- R1 at 12mo: continues, possible acceleration if regional outcomes shift
- R1 at 36mo: phase deepens, multipolarity more visible
- R1 at 5-10yr: phase resolves toward new structure (multipolar with US still strongest)

(Similar projections for R2, R3, R4)

### Stems (with projected evolution):
- Stem A "Restore American hegemonic posture" → strengthens through 36mo, peaks ~2028
- Stem B "Saudi/Israel regional dominance" → strengthens 12-36mo, plateaus
- Stem C "Military operations as economic instrument" → strengthens, normalizes

### Branches (projected):
- Defense procurement branch: accelerates 12mo (orders flowing now)
- Information operations branch: continues
- Economic warfare branch: continues, expands
- Coalition coordination branch: degrades (NATO fissures spread)

### Fruits and probabilities:

**Fruit 1: Multi-decade Pentagon contracts (ripening)**
- Already partially ripe (signed contracts)
- Probability of full ripening: 90% (only catastrophic political shift would cancel)
- Time horizon: 10-20 years revenue visible by 2030
- Beneficiaries: RTX (most exposure to Tomahawk replacement, ~$40B over 10y), LMT (THAAD x4 production, ~$25B), NOC (B-2 ops, ISR, ~$15B)
- Conditional dependencies: maintained Pentagon budget, no major treaty restrictions
- Seeds: yes, R&D funding for next-gen systems (PrSM, hypersonics) — high seeding capacity

**Fruit 2: Hormuz blockade precedent (forming)**
- Forming through 2026
- Probability of becoming durable doctrine: 70%
- Time horizon: doctrinal effect 10-30 years
- Beneficiaries (positive): future US administrations seeking economic coercion
- Beneficiaries (negative): BRICS+ nations accelerating dedollarization
- Seeds: BRICS+ alternative settlement system, probability 60% of meaningful germination by 2028, possible acceleration of yuan oil settlement

**Fruit 3: Saudi-Israeli regional dominance (ripening)**
- Already partially ripe
- Probability of full ripening: 75%
- Time horizon: 10+ years of regional configuration
- Beneficiaries: MBS personally, House of Saud, Netanyahu coalition, UAE leadership
- Conditional: continued US backing or non-interference
- Seeds: Abraham Accords expansion to additional Arab states, probability 40%

**Fruit 4: Iranian leadership decapitation effect (forming)**
- Forming, will be visible 2027-2028
- Probability of durable transformation: 50%
- Time horizon: 5-10 years restructured Iranian politics
- Beneficiaries: Iranian pragmatic factions, Saudi/Israel
- Seeds: possible Iran-Saudi normalization (low probability ~15% but high seed value)

**Fruit 5: NATO trust erosion (forming, ambiguous)**
- Forming, full effect 5-15 years
- Probability of becoming durable: 65%
- Beneficiaries (negative): future US administrations face higher coordination costs
- Beneficiaries (positive): CSTO, SCO gain relative attractiveness
- Seeds: alternative alliance architectures, multi-polar security frameworks, probability 75% of meaningful structural change

### Divergence branches (from two-paths-synthesis):

**Divergence point 1: Iran regime succession (Path A: stable transition; Path B: engineered acceleration)**
- Path A (probability 60%): smooth succession of Khamenei's son, gradual moderation
  - Sub-fruits: gradual Iran-Saudi normalization, possible Iran reintegration into oil markets in 5-7 years
- Path B (probability 40%): factional conflict, possible regime structural change
  - Sub-fruits: more volatile region, larger defense fruits, but also higher acceleration of dedollarization

**Divergence point 2: NATO continuation (Path A: NATO survives in modified form; Path B: NATO bifurcates)**
- Path A (probability 70%): NATO continues with reduced ambition
  - Sub-fruits: defense companies retain Western markets
- Path B (probability 30%): NATO bifurcates between US-aligned and EU-autonomous wings
  - Sub-fruits: European defense industry boom (Rheinmetall, BAE, Dassault, Saab) — much larger fruit than under Path A

### Falsifiable indicators (with dates):

- ITA ETF closes ≥15% above current level by Dec 31, 2026 → confirms Fruit 1 ripening trajectory
- RTX revenue from Tomahawk replacement orders disclosed in 2026 annual report ≥$3B in new bookings → confirms Fruit 1 specifically
- Any BRICS+ joint announcement on oil settlement currency by Dec 31, 2027 → confirms Seed within Fruit 2 germinating
- Saudi-Iran ambassador-level relations restored by Dec 31, 2027 → confirms Path A on Divergence 1
- Major NATO member explicit threat to leave or restructure NATO by Dec 31, 2027 → confirms Path B on Divergence 2
- European defense ETF (EUAD or equivalent) outperforms ITA by 20%+ over 24 months → confirms Path B on Divergence 2 with European fruit interpretation

### Tree summary:

The forecast identifies 5 ripening or forming fruits with probabilities and beneficiaries. The most certain (probability 90%) is the Pentagon procurement fruit feeding RTX/LMT/NOC over 10-20 years. The most asymmetric is Path B of Divergence 2 — if NATO bifurcates, European defense companies (Rheinmetall, BAE, etc.) become fruits 5-10x larger than current pricing reflects.

For investment purposes: the high-confidence fruits are already partially priced in. The asymmetric value lives in the seeds (BRICS+ alternative settlement, Iran-Saudi normalization) and in the divergence-conditional fruits (European defense if NATO bifurcates). These have lower probabilities but vastly higher payoffs if they materialize.

## Anti-patterns

- **Forecasting only ripe fruits.** Easy fruits (already in tree, well-known) are usually priced in. Real value is in forming fruits and seeds.
- **Ignoring seeds.** Seeds have the highest asymmetric value but are the hardest to identify. Skipping the seed analysis means missing the most valuable opportunities.
- **Probability without specificity.** "60% probability of regional restructuring" — too vague to verify. Probabilities should attach to specific fruits with specific beneficiaries on specific time horizons.
- **Single-point estimates without ranges.** Real forecasts have ranges. A fruit's full ripening might be 60-80% probability, not "70%". Pretending precision invites overconfidence.
- **No falsifiable indicators.** A forecast you can't verify is a forecast you can't learn from. Specific binary indicators with dates are mandatory.
- **Forgetting two-paths divergences.** Divergent points become explicit branching nodes, not just text caveats. Different sub-trees may have very different fruits.
- **Beneficiary abstraction.** "Defense industry benefits" is not actionable. Naming RTX, LMT, NOC with specific revenue estimates is.
- **Neglecting time horizon mismatch.** A 30-year fruit trajectory in a tree where roots are projected 36 months — mismatch. Roots must be projected at horizons consistent with fruit ripening.

## Integration with deconstruction protocol

This is **Phase 4** of the deconstruction protocol — the final synthesis. It receives input from all prior phases and produces the actionable forecast that goes both to the chat summary (compact) and to the archive (full).

The chat summary (15-25 lines for the owner) extracts:
- Top 3 fruits with probabilities and beneficiaries
- Top 1-2 seeds with asymmetric upside
- 3-5 most critical falsifiable indicators

The archive entry contains the full tree, all projections, all indicators.

Output is stored in Analysis record under `decisionTreeForecast` field. Verification cycle activates indicator dates: when an indicator's date arrives, the verifier checks fact vs prediction and records the result, feeding into Brier score calculation and learning loops.
